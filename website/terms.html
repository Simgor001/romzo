5. 数据与安全

5. Data and security

测试节点不提供保密或重要数据的托管。不要发送口令、私钥、身份证件、支付资料等敏感信息。HTTPS/WSS 只加密传输通道，应用负载是否加密由接入应用决定。

为排障、控制资源消耗和防止滥用，节点会记录来源 IP、房间号、连接时间、连接数、频道数及入站和出站字节数。开启创建 IP 记录后，还会保存房间创建请求的来源 IP。IP 可能多人共用，不能单独用来认定个人身份。

房间运行记录仅供持有管理密钥的运营人员查看，不公开展示，也不回传给开源项目方；监控不记录消息正文。房间结束 24 小时后运行记录自动清除，关联文件在下一次保存时清理。

运营人员可以让房间下线、断开其连接并拒绝恢复票据。房间恢复时，若创建记录已不存在，恢复者的 IP 不会被当作创建 IP。

自托管节点的数据处理由其运营者自行说明。运营者处理反馈、排障或依法配合调查时，只会在必要且合法的范围内使用和披露数据。

Do not send passwords, private keys, identity documents, payment details or other sensitive information through this relay; it does not host confidential or important data. HTTPS/WSS encrypts transport only; whether application payloads are encrypted is up to the application.

For troubleshooting, resource control and abuse prevention, the relay records source IPs, room codes, connection times, connection and channel counts, and inbound/outbound byte totals. With creator-IP recording enabled, it also stores the source IP of the room-allocation request. IPs may be shared and do not by themselves identify a person.

Room records are accessible only to operators holding the management key. They are not displayed publicly or shared with the open-source project, and monitoring does not record message bodies. Room records are deleted 24 hours after the room ends; associated files are cleaned up at the next save.

Operators may take a room offline, disconnect its clients and reject restore tickets. If a room’s creation record no longer exists, the restorer’s IP is not treated as the creator IP.

Self-hosted operators define their own data handling. When handling feedback, troubleshooting or lawful investigations, the operator uses and discloses data only to the extent necessary and lawful.

8. Feedback

节点无需注册，不核验使用者真实身份。反馈问题时可提供发生时间、相关房号和问题描述，供我们定位故障或滥用；我们不承诺识别相关使用者的真实身份。请勿在公开 Issue 中提交个人信息、票据、私钥或完整消息记录。私密反馈渠道将在确认后补充。
